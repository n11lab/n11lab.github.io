2. Professional Audits, VPAT Reports & Compliance Disclaimers

N-Labs conducts exhaustive manual and automated accessibility testing in strict conformance with W3C WCAG 2.0, 2.1, and 2.2 Levels A, AA, and AAA, Section 508, GIGW, and EN 301 549 benchmarks. However, clients and stakeholders must note the following operational parameters:

  • Point-in-Time Evaluation: An audit report or Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT / ACR) represents an evaluation of a specific software release, DOM state, or URL branch on the date testing was executed. Ongoing code changes by third-party teams may affect conformance.
  • Legal Advice Disclaimer: While our audits are designed to mitigate litigation risks under ADA Title III, Section 508, and the RPwD Act 2016, our findings constitute technical conformance assessments and do not constitute formal legal counsel. Organizations should consult qualified legal counsel for statutory defense.
  • Remediation Implementation: N-Labs provides developer-ready code snippets and technical architectural guidance; however, final implementation, compilation, and regression testing within the client's production code remain the responsibility of the client's engineering team.

3. Electronic Document Remediation (PDF/UA ISO 14289)

Document remediation services (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint) delivered under the Accessible Document Specialist (ADS) domain ensure full tag tree structuring, alternative text tagging, and screen reader reading order.

Clients are responsible for ensuring they possess the legal rights, copyrights, or institutional clearance to share documents for remediation. N-Labs executes non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) to protect document confidentiality and deletes working copies upon delivery confirmation.
