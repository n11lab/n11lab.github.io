Institutional Privacy Policy & Data Protection Charter

At N-Labs (Nitesh Inclusion Labs), privacy is held to the exact same uncompromising standard as digital accessibility: it is an essential civil right and foundational human infrastructure. Directed by Nitesh Kumar, our organization maintains an absolute policy of transparency, data minimization, and technical integrity.

This document explains clearly, in plain language, how data is processed when you visit our portal, utilize our interactive audit engines, enroll in educational academies, or commission commercial accessibility audits.

Effective Date: Friday, October 9, 2026 | Document Version: 2.1-PRIVACY

1. Zero Third-Party Tracking & Cookie-Less Architecture

Unlike conventional corporate websites, N-Labs operates on a strict zero tracking cookie policy:

  • No Commercial Ad Trackers: We do not embed Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, marketing beacons, or surveillance trackers of any kind.
  • No Third-Party Font CDNs: Typography styles (including system fonts and OpenDyslexic fallbacks) are handled directly through standard native stacks without transmitting your IP to external providers.
  • No Cross-Site Profiling: Your browsing habits across this portal are never compiled, packaged, or sold to advertising networks or data brokers.

2. Local-First Session Telemetry & Preferences

You will notice an active Terminal Access Telemetry module in our footer and an accessibility preferences toolbar at the top of the page. Here is exactly how this data is handled:

  • Storage Location: All telemetry data—including visit counter, prior visit timestamp, text zoom level, high-contrast toggle state, and OpenDyslexic font choice—is saved exclusively within your browser's private localStorage.
  • Zero Server Transmission: This telemetry information resides only on your local hardware device. It is never transmitted across the network, stored in a backend database, or accessed by outside parties.
  • User Control: You can completely erase this data at any moment simply by clearing your browser's local cache or clicking the "Reset Default View" button on our accessibility toolbar.

3. Commercial Client Audit Confidentiality & NDAs

Organizations commissioning web/mobile WCAG audits, PDF document remediation, or VPAT/ACR evaluations entrust N-Labs with proprietary digital assets:

  • Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs): We execute bilateral NDAs prior to accessing staging environments, pre-production code repositories, or proprietary document archives.
  • Secure Handling: Client credentials and software source code provided for accessibility inspection are stored in encrypted, isolated environments accessible strictly to Nitesh Kumar.
  • Data Purging: Upon delivery of the final audit report, VPAT template, or remediated PDF files and client acceptance, all temporary files and staging credentials are permanently purged within 30 calendar days.

4. Student Admission & Course Registration Data

When you register for Course 1 (Web Development Basics - ₹99) or Course 2 (Auditor Masterclass - ₹299) via our register.html portal:

  • Information Collected: We collect only necessary details: your full name, email address, city/state, course choice, and preferred assistive technology.
  • Purpose of Collection: This data is utilized solely to schedule your Google Meet training batches, dispatch course joining links, and issue your official N-Labs completion certificate.
  • No Spam Policy: Your contact email will never be enrolled in automated promotional marketing lists or shared with third-party education portals.

5. Interactive Audit Engines: Zero Server Retention

The 5 interactive accessibility engines hosted on tools.html (URL Conformance Auditor, HTML Component Inspector, Document Tree Auditor, Contrast Verifier, and Landmark Checker) execute purely on the client side using your web browser's local JavaScript runtime.

Any website URLs, HTML code snippets, or document extracts you paste into these tools are processed locally in memory. None of your pasted code or text leaves your computer or passes through an external server.

6. Statutory Compliance & Your Legal Rights

This Privacy Policy conforms to national and global privacy mandates, including:

  • India: The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023).
  • European Union: The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation EU 2016/679).
  • United States: The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA).

You retain the unconditional right to request information regarding any personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request complete erasure by writing directly to our official directorate inbox.
