Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the official knowledge repository of N-Labs (Nitesh Inclusion Labs). Here you will find definitive answers concerning our manual auditing procedures, document remediation services, training curricula, pricing structures, and compliance mandates.

Each response is grounded in our operational expertise across W3C WCAG 2.1/2.2 Levels A, AA, and AAA, WAI-ARIA 1.2, and PDF/UA (ISO 14289) standards.

1. Accessibility Audits, Methodology & VPAT / ACR

Q: Why is an automated accessibility scan insufficient for true compliance?
Automated tools (such as axe-core or Lighthouse) are valuable for syntax sweeps, but they can only detect approximately 30% to 40% of WCAG violations. Critical barriers—such as keyboard focus traps, logical reading order, context-appropriate alternative text, accessible names on custom widgets, and dynamic screen reader announcements—require lived, manual inspection using native assistive devices (NVDA, JAWS, TalkBack).
Q: What deliverables does N-Labs provide after an enterprise audit?
Our audits yield three primary deliverables:
  1. Executive Conformance Summary: High-level compliance status mapped to WCAG 2.1/2.2 Levels A, AA, and AAA.
  2. Technical Defect Sheet: Prioritized tickets with exact steps to reproduce, screen reader speech outputs, and working developer remediation code.
  3. Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT / ACR): Standardized accessibility conformance report ready for enterprise procurement and government tenders.
Q: What assistive technologies and operating systems are used during testing?
We test across dual environments: desktop testing using NVDA and JAWS on Windows with Chrome and Firefox; and mobile testing using TalkBack on Android and VoiceOver on iOS/macOS with Safari. All keyboard-only interactions are evaluated with mice unplugged.

2. Electronic Document Remediation (PDF/UA ISO 14289)

Q: What is PDF/UA and why does a standard PDF fail screen reader testing?
PDF/UA (ISO 14289-1) is the international standard for universal electronic document accessibility. Standard PDFs often lack a semantic tag tree, meaning screen readers perceive them as flat graphics or jumbled characters. Remediation introduces structural tags for headings (<H1>–<H6>), paragraphs (<P>), tables, lists, and artifacts, guaranteeing that assistive tech reads content in proper logical sequence.
Q: Which document file formats does N-Labs remediate?
We remediate Adobe PDF, Microsoft Word (.docx), Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), and Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx) documents. Complex tables with multi-level headers, mathematical equations, and technical diagrams receive manual tag architecture.

4. Global Legal Compliance & Risk Mitigation

Q: Which legal compliance frameworks apply to digital products?
Depending on geographic distribution and sector, platforms must comply with:
  • United States: ADA Title III (commercial digital spaces) and Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act (government and federally funded software).
  • European Union: The European Accessibility Act (EAA / EN 301 549) enforcing mandatory compliance across e-commerce, banking, and public utilities.
  • India: The Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act 2016 and the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0), requiring adherence to WCAG AA benchmarks.
Q: How quickly can N-Labs begin an enterprise auditing project?
Upon receiving your URL scope or document volume via our contact portal, we provide a formal evaluation and timeline within 24 to 48 business hours. Testing begins immediately following mutual scope confirmation and non-disclosure agreement (NDA) execution.
