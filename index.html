Executive Mandate & Mission

Welcome to the official engineering portal of N-Labs (Nitesh Inclusion Labs), an international digital accessibility consultancy founded and directed by Nitesh Kumar. Operating with complete personal visual impairment, Nitesh combines authentic daily dependence on assistive technology (NVDA, TalkBack, JAWS, Braille displays) with global compliance frameworks to eliminate digital discrimination.

We audit, remediate, and certify enterprise web applications, mobile platforms, and electronic documents in strict conformance with W3C WCAG 2.0, 2.1, and 2.2 Levels A, AA, and AAA, Section 508 of the US Rehabilitation Act, the European Accessibility Act (EN 301 549), and India’s GIGW directives.
