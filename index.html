Accessibility Knowledge Base & Audit Reports

1. Overcoming the Limitations of Automated Scanners

By Nitesh Kumar | Subject: Manual Auditing Protocols

While automated tools (axe DevTools, Lighthouse) are efficient for detecting syntax-level errors like missing image descriptions or color contrast drops, they miss over 60% of real-world usability issues. Only an experienced screen reader user can verify meaningful reading sequences, form error contexts, and dynamic state updates.

2. Designing for Cross-Disability Inclusivity

By Nitesh Kumar | Subject: Universal Design & WCAG 2.2

Inclusivity extends beyond blindness. It encompasses low vision users needing 400% zoom reflow, motor-impaired individuals navigating via single switch keys, and neurodiverse users who need predictable navigation patterns and distraction-free layouts.
