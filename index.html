Executive Mission & Platform Mandate

Welcome to the primary digital architecture of Nitesh Inclusion Labs (N-Labs), an international digital accessibility testing and technical research consultancy founded and engineered by Nitesh Kumar. Operating with total visual impairment, Nitesh unites daily assistive device reliance (NVDA, JAWS, TalkBack, Braille displays) with global compliance frameworks to engineer barrier-free digital environments.

N-Labs audits, remediates, and certifies enterprise software, government portals, and educational ecosystems in strict conformance with W3C WCAG 2.0, 2.1, and 2.2 Levels A, AA, and AAA, Section 508 of the US Rehabilitation Act, the European Accessibility Act (EN 301 549), and India’s GIGW directives.
