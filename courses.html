Professional Training Academies

Welcome to the learning wing of N-Labs. Our training programs are designed to eliminate systemic employment barriers by providing high-demand technical capabilities at accessible price points. Taught directly by Nitesh Kumar, these courses cater to visually impaired individuals, persons with disabilities across all categories, and aspiring quality assurance auditors.

Classes are conducted live via Google Meet in Hindi Medium with flexible, always-open scheduling so you can balance your learning with your personal routines.

Foundational Track Investment: ₹99 Only

Course 1: Complete HTML5, WAI-ARIA & Accessible Web Basics

A comprehensive coding foundation designed to take learners from absolute zero to mastering semantic web authoring and accessible UI patterns.

Core Program Metadata

  • Course Fee: ₹99 (One-time, non-recurring)
  • Duration: 6 Weeks (Self-paced practical exercises + live mentoring)
  • Medium of Instruction: Hindi Medium
  • Delivery Mode: Live Interactive Sessions via Google Meet
  • Eligibility: Open to all categories of learners, including screen reader users and beginners.
  • Certification: Official N-Labs Certificate of Completion.

Comprehensive Curriculum & Topics Covered

Module 1: HTML5 Architecture (From Scratch to Advanced)

  • Document skeletons, DOCTYPE, metadata, and language attributes.
  • Semantic text: Headings (H1–H6), paragraphs, lists, blockquotes, and inline tags.
  • Semantic data tables: Caption, thead, tbody, th with scope attributes.
  • Form engineering: Accessible inputs, labels, fieldsets, legends, and validation states.

Module 2: Complete WAI-ARIA 1.2 Standards

  • The 5 Golden Rules of ARIA and avoiding common anti-patterns.
  • The 5 Core Landmarks: banner, navigation, main, complementary, and contentinfo.
  • Essential widget attributes: aria-expanded, aria-controls, aria-hidden, aria-current.
  • Live regions: Designing non-intrusive alerts with aria-live="polite".

Module 3: Essential Practical CSS for Accessibility

  • WCAG 1.4.3 color contrast minimums and relative luminance calculations.
  • Visible keyboard focus styling using :focus-visible and high-contrast rings.
  • Fluid responsive layouts using Flexbox and Grid without breaking zoom reflow.
  • High-contrast modes and dyslexic typography integration.

Module 4: Essential JavaScript for Accessible UI

  • DOM selection and event listeners (keyboard vs. mouse).
  • Managing keyboard traps inside modal dialogs and dropdown menus.
  • Dynamic ARIA state toggling via vanilla JavaScript.
  • Focus restoration principles (returning focus to trigger buttons).

Professional QA Track Investment: ₹299 Only

Course 2: Professional Digital Accessibility Tester & Auditor Masterclass

An intensive, career-oriented masterclass built upon the combined curricula of IAAP CPACC, Deque University, NAB Delhi DADCA, and ADS frameworks. Learn to audit enterprise software and prepare for global testing roles.

Core Program Metadata

  • Course Fee: ₹299 (One-time, non-recurring)
  • Duration: 10 Weeks (Intensive hands-on auditing & defect documentation)
  • Medium of Instruction: Hindi Medium
  • Delivery Mode: Live Hands-on Auditing via Google Meet
  • Eligibility: Aspiring QA testers, developers, visually impaired professionals, and accessibility champions.
  • Certification: Certified N-Labs Accessibility Auditor (NAA) Credential.

Comprehensive Curriculum & Topics Covered

Module 1: The WCAG 2.1 & 2.2 Auditing Matrix

  • Deep dive into the 4 Principles (POUR), 13 Guidelines, and 86 Success Criteria.
  • Conformance Levels A, AA, and AAA: What enterprise clients demand.
  • New WCAG 2.2 Criteria: Focus appearance, target size minimums, and accessible authentication.
  • Global compliance laws: Section 508, ADA Title III, European Accessibility Act (EN 301 549), and India's GIGW / RPwD Act.

Module 2: Advanced Manual Screen Reader QA

  • NVDA power-testing: Virtual buffer navigation, Speech Viewer logging, and rotor keys.
  • TalkBack mobile testing on Android: Touch gestures, linear navigation, and local context menus.
  • Keyboard-only navigation protocols: Tab order, focus loops, and modal dismissals.
  • Dual-engine verification: Combining automated sweeps (axe-core) with manual inspection.

Module 3: Enterprise Defect Reporting & Jira Integration

  • Writing production-ready Jira defect tickets with reproducible steps.
  • Documenting screen reader actual vs. expected speech feedback.
  • Supplying working developer remediation snippets to resolve tickets faster.
  • Managing defect triage and severity classifications (Blocker vs. Minor).

Module 4: VPAT Evaluation & Document Remediation (ADS Domain)

  • Reading and evaluating Voluntary Product Accessibility Templates (VPAT / ACR).
  • PDF/UA (ISO 14289) basics: Heading trees, table header scope, and reading order fixes.
  • Word and Excel document accessibility remediation techniques.
  • Portfolio building: Conducting and publishing your first end-to-end live website audit.
