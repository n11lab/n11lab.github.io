Enterprise Digital Accessibility Services

At N-Labs (Nitesh Inclusion Labs), digital accessibility is approached as mission-critical civil engineering. Under the direct technical supervision of Nitesh Kumar, our practice provides organizations with an authentic, dual-engine methodology: combining rigorous automated syntax sweeps with expert manual screen reader evaluations.

Whether your goal is satisfying international compliance mandates (ADA Title III, Section 508, European Accessibility Act EN 301 549, GIGW 3.0) or unlocking full product operability for disabled users, our 5 core services deliver verified results.

1. Comprehensive Web & Mobile Accessibility Auditing

Automated checkers miss over 60% of real-world barriers. Our full-scale auditing service delivers exhaustive evaluation across public websites, enterprise SaaS applications, and native mobile platforms (Android and iOS).

Scope & Methodology

  • Standards Evaluated: W3C WCAG 2.0, 2.1, and 2.2 across Levels A, AA, and AAA; Section 508; GIGW.
  • Dual-Engine Testing: Automated code scanning (axe-core, Lighthouse) coupled with 100% manual assistive interaction.
  • Assistive Tech Stack: NVDA and JAWS on Chromium/Firefox (Windows), VoiceOver on Safari (macOS/iOS), TalkBack on Chrome (Android).
  • Keyboard Only Verification: Systematic validation of tab order, focus indicators, modal dialog traps, and bypass mechanisms.

Deliverables Provided

A prioritized Executive Conformance Summary, a developer-ready Defect Sheet (with exact steps to reproduce and code snippets), and an official Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT / ACR) ready for enterprise procurement.

2. PDF & Electronic Document Remediation (PDF/UA ISO 14289)

Inaccessible documents represent an invisible barrier across corporate annual reports, public policy notices, educational handouts, and banking statements. Grounded in the Accessible Document Specialist (ADS) domain, N-Labs remediates high-volume document archives to full universal readability.

Scope & Methodology

  • Supported Formats: Adobe PDF, Microsoft Word (.docx), Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx).
  • Structural Tagging: Establishing logical document heading trees (H1–H6), paragraphs, lists, and artifact tags for decorative elements.
  • Complex Data Tables: Remediation of multi-tier tabular data with explicit row/column headers and scope mapping.
  • Alternative Text Formulation: Crafting concise, context-aware descriptions for charts, diagrams, and organizational logos.
  • Compliance Verification: PAC (PDF Accessibility Checker) validation alongside manual NVDA/TalkBack reading verification.

Deliverables Provided

100% tagged, certified accessible documents conforming to PDF/UA (ISO 14289) and WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA document standards.

Request Document Remediation →

3. Technical Developer Remediation & WAI-ARIA Engineering

Traditional audit reports frequently overwhelm developers with compliance jargon without providing actionable code. N-Labs bridges the engineering gap by working directly within your code repository to supply clean, standards-compliant solutions.

Scope & Methodology

  • Semantic HTML5 Refactoring: Replacing generic clickable divs with native semantic buttons, anchors, and form elements.
  • WAI-ARIA 1.2 Implementation: Correct application of landmark roles, aria-expanded, aria-controls, aria-live, and modal dialog patterns.
  • Focus Management Architecture: Writing vanilla JavaScript routines to handle focus trapping in popups and restoring focus on dismissal.
  • Custom Component Design: Engineering custom accordions, tabsets, carousels, and complex grids that mirror W3C APG patterns.

Deliverables Provided

Copy-paste production code snippets, pull request reviews, and direct consultations with your front-end development leads.

Hire Technical Consulting →

4. Corporate Accessibility Training & SOP Governance

Building sustainable digital inclusion requires shifting left—empowering your product managers, UI/UX designers, and quality assurance engineers to incorporate accessibility into every sprint of the software development lifecycle (SDLC).

Scope & Methodology

  • Executive Briefings: Understanding global compliance litigation, ADA risk mitigation, and the business ROI of universal access.
  • UI/UX Design Workshops: Color contrast rules, target sizing, non-color visual cues, and accessible wireframing.
  • QA & Tester Enablement: Practical keyboard-only test scripts, installing and operating NVDA for automated sprint checks.
  • Accessibility SOP Development: Crafting organization-wide Standard Operating Procedures and definition-of-done checklists.

Deliverables Provided

Interactive live training workshops via Google Meet, recorded session modules, customized QA checklists, and corporate design guides.

Schedule Corporate Training →

5. Native Assistive Device User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

Compliance on paper does not guarantee authentic usability. N-Labs conducts authentic User Acceptance Testing performed directly by native screen reader power users who interact with assistive computing systems on a daily basis.

Scope & Methodology

  • Real-World Task Completion: Validating critical user journeys including sign-up, complex checkouts, banking transfers, and form submissions.
  • Friction & Cognitive Load Analysis: Identifying verbose ARIA announcements, erratic focus shifts, and disruptive live region spamming.
  • Cross-Platform Compatibility: Verifying identical functional dignity across mobile (Android/iOS) and desktop platforms.

Deliverables Provided

Screen-recorded assistive testing videos with live spoken commentary, non-visual friction logs, and qualitative user experience feedback.
