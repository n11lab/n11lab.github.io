Institutional Profile & Overview

N-Labs (Nitesh Inclusion Labs) is an independent digital accessibility research, software auditing, and assistive engineering consultancy. Headquartered in Masaurhi (Patna district, Bihar), the organization was established to solve a critical, systemic flaw in modern digital products: the wide gap between surface-level automated accessibility compliance and genuine screen reader operability.

While conventional compliance audits frequently rely on superficial automated checkers, N-Labs operates on a philosophy of dual-engine validation. We bridge visual design systems with programmatic assistive technology trees, ensuring that web platforms, mobile applications, and electronic document repositories provide complete functional independence and dignity to every human being.

Strategic Vision

To build a barrier-free digital civilization where every website, application, and electronic document is born accessible by design, guaranteeing equal autonomy, participation, and dignity for all individuals worldwide.

Our vision is anchored in the principle that accessibility is not a cosmetic enhancement or an afterthought; it is fundamental digital architecture and a cornerstone of modern civil rights.

Operational Mission

To translate our vision into measurable results, N-Labs executes across three focused commitments:

Mandate 01

Rigorous Manual Auditing

We conduct comprehensive manual audits on live software using native screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, TalkBack) and keyboard-only interaction. We identify real functional obstacles—such as focus traps and broken ARIA patterns—that automated algorithms miss entirely.

Mandate 02

Actionable Code Remediation

We do not merely report compliance errors. We partner directly with software engineering teams, delivering clean, production-ready semantic HTML5 markup, WAI-ARIA 1.2 implementations, and JavaScript focus management patterns that resolve defects permanently at the source.

Mandate 03

Educational Empowerment

We democratize technical accessibility knowledge through low-cost training programs conducted in Hindi via Google Meet. We train aspiring disabled professionals, QA testers, and front-end developers to build, test, and champion universal inclusion independently.

Founder Journey: The Lived Perspective of Nitesh Kumar

The mission of N-Labs is deeply connected to the personal and technical journey of its founder, Nitesh Kumar. Navigating life with 100% total visual impairment in Masaurhi, Patna, Nitesh experienced firsthand the immense digital barriers present across modern software systems. For him, screen-reading tools such as NVDA, TalkBack, and JAWS are not testing emulators or developer plug-ins; they are his primary interface with the digital world.

When an online public service portal lacks programmatic label associations, an e-commerce checkout button has no accessible name, or a critical PDF document lacks a tagged reading tree, it creates an insurmountable barrier that restricts education, employment, and personal independence. Recognizing that over 60% of real-world usability defects escape automated accessibility checkers, Nitesh transformed his lived operational experience into a rigorous engineering discipline.

Nitesh pursued formal computer applications training at the National Association for the Blind (NAB) Delhi in R.K. Puram, completing the Diploma in Assistive Devices in Computer Applications (DADCA). During this intensive program, he mastered non-visual keyboard interaction, operating system internals, and assistive device configurations. Simultaneously, he earned the Career Edge IT Certification from the NIIT Foundation, graduating with a distinguished score of 94%.

To align his practical skills with international legal and architectural standards, Nitesh completed professional curricula via Deque University and the International Association of Accessibility Professionals (IAAP). He systematically completed the IAAP CPACC (Certified Professional in Accessibility Core Competencies) Track, the Web Accessibility Fundamentals Track, and the Accessible Document Specialist (ADS) Track.

Today, Nitesh combines lived non-visual operational fluency with formal international compliance engineering (W3C WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 Levels A, AA, AAA, Section 508, GIGW). Through N-Labs, he provides software creators with authentic assurance that their platforms do not simply achieve theoretical compliance on paper, but deliver genuine usability, dignity, and independence to every user in practice.
