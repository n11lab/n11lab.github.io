Institutional Profile & Overview

N-Labs (Nitesh Inclusion Labs) is an independent digital accessibility research, software auditing, and assistive engineering consultancy. Headquartered in Masaurhi (Patna district, Bihar), the organization was established to address a persistent, structural defect in modern information technology: the disconnect between technical software code and genuine screen reader operability.

While modern software organizations frequently rely on surface-level automated checkers, N-Labs operates on a philosophy of dual-engine validation. We bridge visual design systems with programmatic assistive trees, ensuring that every digital asset—from enterprise web applications and mobile platforms to public sector portals and PDF documents—functions seamlessly for all human beings, regardless of visual, motor, auditory, or cognitive abilities.

Strategic Vision

To build a barrier-free digital civilization where every website, application, and electronic document is born accessible by design, guaranteeing equal dignity, autonomy, and participation for all individuals across the globe.

Our vision is rooted in the certainty that accessibility is not an optional aesthetic addition or a temporary corporate convenience; it is the fundamental architectural pillar of modern civil rights and digital democracy.

Operational Mission

To transform our strategic vision into measurable, everyday reality, N-Labs executes across three distinct operational mandates:

01

Rigorous Manual Auditing & Verification

We conduct comprehensive manual audits on live software systems using native screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, TalkBack) and keyboard-only navigation. We identify real functional obstacles—such as focus traps, broken name calculations, and missing live regions—that automated algorithms fail to capture.

02

Actionable Code Remediation & Engineering

We do not merely hand over generic bug lists. We partner directly with software engineering teams, delivering clean, production-ready semantic HTML5 markup, WAI-ARIA 1.2 implementations, and JavaScript focus management patterns that resolve defects permanently at the root source.

03

Educational Empowerment & Capacity Building

We democratize technical accessibility knowledge through targeted, low-cost training programs conducted in Hindi via Google Meet. We train aspiring disabled professionals, QA engineers, and designers to build, test, and advocate for universal inclusion independently.

Founder Journey: The Lived Perspective of Nitesh Kumar

The foundation of N-Labs is inseparable from the personal and technical journey of its founder, Nitesh Kumar. Living with 100% total visual impairment in Masaurhi, Patna, Nitesh experienced firsthand the immense digital barriers present across modern computer systems. For him, screen-reading software such as NVDA, TalkBack, and JAWS are not testing simulations or developer plug-ins; they are his primary sensory windows into the digital world.

When an online government form lacks programmatic label associations, an e-commerce checkout button has no accessible name, or a PDF document lacks a tagged reading tree, it does not create a minor annoyance—it creates an insurmountable wall that completely blocks independence, education, and employment. Recognizing that over 60% of real-world usability defects escape automated accessibility checkers, Nitesh chose to turn his lived operational reality into a technical discipline.

Nitesh pursued rigorous formal computer applications training at the prestigious National Association for the Blind (NAB) Delhi in R.K. Puram, completing the Diploma in Assistive Devices in Computer Applications (DADCA). During this intensive program, he mastered non-visual keyboard interaction, operating system internals, and assistive device configurations. Simultaneously, he earned the Career Edge IT Certification from the NIIT Foundation, graduating with a distinguished score of 94%.

To align his practical skills with international legal and architectural standards, Nitesh completed professional curricula via Deque University and the International Association of Accessibility Professionals (IAAP). He systematically completed the IAAP CPACC (Certified Professional in Accessibility Core Competencies) Track, the Web Accessibility Fundamentals Track, and the Accessible Document Specialist (ADS) Track.

Today, Nitesh combines deep lived non-visual operational fluency with formal international compliance engineering (W3C WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 Levels A, AA, AAA, Section 508, GIGW). Through N-Labs, he offers software creators authentic assurance that their platforms do not just pass automated compliance on paper, but provide seamless dignity, operability, and independence to every user in practice.
